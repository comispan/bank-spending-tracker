Cards

One row per card the statements were billed to. When a bank replaces a card — reissued after fraud, renewed on expiry, upgraded — the new number arrives here as a new card, because nothing in the statement says the two are the same card. Saying so below folds them into one line on Months and Analytics.

This is not cosmetic. A month counts as complete only when every card is billed for all of it, so an unlinked replacement leaves a card missing from every month — the one that stopped, or the one that had not started — and takes the like-for-like comparison and the trailing average with it.

It changes labels, not data. Each statement stays on the card that actually billed it, no transaction moves, and unlinking puts both cards back on the timeline exactly as they were.
