Spending by month

Bucketed by transaction date, never by which statement a row arrived in — your cards close between the 13th and the 20th, so every calendar month draws on two statements per card. The newest month is therefore always part-billed, which is why each row says how much of itself it can actually account for. A month total without that caveat is not a small error: it reads as a collapse in spending that never happened.
