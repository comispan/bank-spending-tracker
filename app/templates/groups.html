Merchant groups

One company, many outlets. sheng siong ss jl and sheng siong supermarket are the same shop billing from two branches, and on a report they should be one line. Grouping them here folds them together in Analytics and on each month page — and every grouped row can still be expanded to show the outlets behind it.

This changes labels, not data. Each transaction keeps its own merchant key, so categories, rules and merchant memory carry on working per outlet exactly as before. Ungrouping puts the rows back with nothing to undo. That is also why nothing is grouped for you: the worst a wrong group can do is add two numbers together, but it is still your call which two.
